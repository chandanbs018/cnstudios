Project Overview

Curating corporate gifting into an effortless digital exploration.

Visionary Gift Studios required a digital catalogue that made exploring corporate gifting products effortless. Rather than creating a conventional ecommerce experience with complex checkout carts and retail friction, the focus was on building a premium digital catalogue that helped businesses quickly discover curated gifting options while reinforcing the company's brand identity.

Corporate procurement teams and HR executives need clarity—instant access to package contents, customization possibilities, and minimum order quantities without wading through 100-page static PDFs.

The experience prioritizes simplicity, visual hierarchy, and accessibility, providing a seamless bridge between online browsing and direct B2B inquiries.

The Challenge

Corporate gifting businesses often struggle with overwhelming product collections. The catalogue had to feel curated rather than crowded — transforming a chaotic product list into an organized, high-end experience that inspires decision-makers.

01

Difficult Product Discovery

Clients were forced to scroll through disjointed PDF catalogues, making finding specific occasion hampers slow and tedious.

02

Inconsistent Presentation

Varying photo quality and disorganized product specifications undermined the premium craftsmanship of the gift sets.

03

Poor Mobile Browsing

Corporate executives reviewing options on mobile devices experienced frustrating pinch-to-zoom layouts and unreadable text.

04

Weak Premium Perception

Without an elevated digital storefront, the brand struggled to differentiate its custom curation from low-cost commodity suppliers.

Our Solution

A catalogue-first digital system designed around speed, elegance, and frictionless discovery.

Designed browsing around clear visual hierarchy, categorized hampers (Festive, Executive, Onboarding, Eco-friendly), and scannable collections.

02

Execution Timeline

How It Was Built

A focused four-stage rollout turning complex corporate inventory into a fluid digital showroom.

01

Taxonomy Strategy

We structured 100+ product options into 4 intuitive categories (Executive, Onboarding, Festive, Eco-Friendly) for immediate scannability.

02

Curation Design

Crafted high-contrast showcase cards highlighting hamper components, bulk MOQs, and custom corporate logo embossing options.

03

Responsive Engineering

Built a rapid client-side filtering system and mobile quotation drawer for seamless access on all screen sizes.

04

Launch & RFQ Funnel

Integrated instant WhatsApp quote triggers and digital catalogue downloads, optimizing the B2B procurement workflow.

Engineering & Craft

Behind the Build

Key strategic decisions that turned a standard inventory list into a high-converting corporate asset.

Catalogue vs Retail Cart

Direct B2B RFQ Architecture

Corporate gifting is driven by custom volumes and bespoke branding. We bypassed standard retail shopping carts in favor of a direct Request-for-Quote (RFQ) model, increasing qualified inquiries by removing checkout friction.

Visual Storytelling

The Luxury Unboxing Experience

Every product card showcases the unpackaged presentation, material craftsmanship, and gift contents, giving procurement heads total confidence before requesting physical samples.

Zero-Latency UX

Instant Client-Side Filtering

Using lightweight vanilla JavaScript without heavy external libraries, category switching occurs in under 16ms with smooth CSS cross-fades and zero layout shift.
