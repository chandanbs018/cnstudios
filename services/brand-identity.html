Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our brand identity design process.

What files do I receive upon completion?

You receive complete master asset folders with vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) for infinite scaling, high-res transparent PNGs, and a PDF brand guidelines document.

Do I own the full intellectual property?

Yes. Once final project payment is settled, 100% full commercial ownership and copyright of the final chosen identity assets are transferred to your company.

How many initial concepts do you provide?

We present 2 to 3 distinct, fully thought-out design directions with real-world mockup applications to help you choose the strongest brand trajectory.

Can you redesign an existing logo?

Yes. We offer brand evolutions (refining and modernizing your existing equity) as well as total ground-up brand transformations.
