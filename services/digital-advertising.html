Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our digital advertising management.

What ad budget should we start with?

We typically recommend a minimum monthly ad spend of ₹40,000 to ₹1,00,000 to ensure sufficient testing volume and rapid algorithmic learning.

Who owns the ad accounts and creative assets?

You own 100% of all ad accounts, pixel data, and creative assets. Everything is run directly inside your company's Meta Business Manager and Google Ads accounts.

How quickly do we see results?

Initial lead flow typically begins within the first 48 to 72 hours of campaign launch, with full algorithmic optimization and lowest acquisition costs settling over weeks 2 through 4.

Do you design the ad creative and write the copy?

Yes. Our team produces all ad graphics, motion banners, direct-response copy, and headline angles as part of the full management service.
