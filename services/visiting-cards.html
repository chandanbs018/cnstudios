Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our printing and collateral production.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Standard business card runs start at 200 units per name. For specialty packaging or custom rigid boxes, MOQs typically start between 50 to 100 units.

What paper thickness do you recommend?

For executive business cards, we recommend 400 GSM soft-touch matte or 600 GSM multi-layer cotton stock for a distinctive, weighty hand-feel.

Do you provide print-ready source files?

Yes. You receive press-ready vector PDFs with embedded fonts, 3mm bleeds, CMYK color profiles, and dedicated spot UV/foil separation vector layers.

Can you handle the printing and physical shipping?

Yes. We manage the entire production lifecycle with our certified pre-press print partners and deliver finished materials directly to your office across India.
