Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our social media marketing engagements.

How often will you post on our accounts?

Standard retainers typically range between 12 to 20 high-value posts per month (3 to 5 posts weekly), focusing on depth and quality over low-effort filler content.

Do we get to approve posts before they go live?

Yes. We deliver the full batch of creative assets, copy, and posting dates at the start of each month so you can approve everything in a single 15-minute review.

Which platforms do you manage?

We primarily focus on Instagram and LinkedIn for B2B and consumer brands, with extended support for YouTube Shorts, X (Twitter), and Facebook.

Can you coordinate organic social with paid ads?

Yes. Because NC Studios handles both organic social and paid digital advertising, we seamlessly turn top-performing organic posts into high-ROAS paid ads.
